How we work

  1. You tell me what you have. In writing or in a free half-hour call.
  2. I send you a proposal with the scope, the price and the delivery date, within two days.
  3. We start with 50%. Every week you get a link to see where it is.
  4. I deliver, you pay the other 50%, and for 30 days I fix any bug at no cost.

I am in Lima (UTC−5), so my working day overlaps with the United States. We can work in English or in Spanish.

Questions

Do you use AI?

Yes, every day. That is why I deliver in weeks what used to take months. What I do not hand to AI: deciding what to build, reviewing what it writes, testing it, and answering when something fails.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The code and the data are yours and I hand them over when we finish. If one day you want someone else to maintain it, they can.

What if something breaks after delivery?

For the first 30 days I fix it at no cost. After that, through the care plan or as a one-off job I quote before doing it.

How do I pay?

By international bank transfer, 50% to start and 50% on delivery. The care plan is billed monthly.
