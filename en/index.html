Systems that keep working when nobody is watching

I am Yoiber, a full-stack developer in Lima, Peru. Backend in Python and Node, interfaces in React and TypeScript, and Linux machines a long way from any keyboard. The Spanish home page is the full one, animations included; this is the short version for readers in English.

Four systems, explained from the inside

Each case covers what the problem was, which decisions it forced and what the result looks like.

  • A Kip-Up product The receipt leaves the system and the tax office accepts it. Peruvian electronic invoicing written from scratch: UBL 2.1, a digital signature and delivery to the tax office. With video.
  • Custom system Twenty modules around the day's schedule. The system for a therapy center: patients, sessions, specialists, electronic invoices and staff.
  • Field operations Man-hours per technician, on every vessel. Every vessel carries a QR code that technicians scan when they board and when they leave, so their hours come from where they really were, ready to bill the client and pay the crew.

Work you can open

Projects from the Spanish home page that you can open right now. Their interfaces are in Spanish.

  • Creator's website · three.js · Anime.jsSebastian Murazzo. A scroll-driven site with a 3D recording kit and a simulated video editor, still in progress.

  • Drama tracker · Svelte · Capacitor · Node · TMDBDaebon. My list of Asian dramas, designed like a Korean script: each show is a stapled script cover, the episodes I still have to watch are highlighted, and each page says which streaming service carries it in Peru. A small Node backend keeps the TMDB key out of the app. Also on Android.
  • Anime tracker · Svelte · Capacitor · AniListTsuzuku. My anime list: the episode I am on, an airing calendar in my time zone and where to watch each show legally. On Android it notifies me when the next episode airs.
  • Android · Kotlin · Health Connectkm 0. A walk and run recorder for when I hit start too late: it pulls the stretch I had already walked from Health Connect and puts the KM 0 marker where I really started.
  • 3D · Svelte · ThrelteNazca. A dawn flight over the Nazca lines, with the hummingbird drawn as a single line that never crosses itself.
  • Live data · Nuxt · Vue · TresJSRemezón. Peru's earthquakes live and in 3D, each one at the depth where it started. Seen from the side, fifty years of earthquakes trace the Nazca plate diving under the Andes.
  • Open source · RustDocuGraph MCP and Kuidy Lyrics. An MCP server that lets an AI agent ask a PDF of thousands of pages and get back the paragraph with its page number, offline; and synced lyrics floating over any Windows window.

What I am good at

Management systems that survive daily use, with the boring parts done properly (permissions, valid invoices, figures that reconcile). And interfaces for live data that do not turn the screen into a Christmas tree.
